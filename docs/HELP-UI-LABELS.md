# UI controls with no visible text label

- **Undo** and **Redo** toolbar buttons use arrow icons; their `title` attributes name the action.
- **Zoom out**, **Zoom in**, **Grid**, **Recover snapshot**, **Project properties**, **Duplicate**, **Rename**, and **Delete** controls are icon-only; tooltips/title attributes identify their actions in the relevant panel.
- The top-right **Settings**, **Command search**, and **Safety and help** controls show ⚙, ⌕, and ? respectively; their accessible labels identify them.
